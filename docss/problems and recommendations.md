## Recommendations

1. **Reduce reliance on São Paulo and the top 3 categories through regional expansion and cross-selling.**
   * High operational and revenue concentration in São Paulo and top product categories creates severe structural vulnerability.
   * Decentralizing merchant acquisition to under-penetrated regional markets and implementing targeted cross-selling strategies will balance out market distribution and mitigate long-haul friction.
2. **Shift CRM spend from acquisition toward post-purchase re-engagement within 14–30 days of delivery.**
   * The data shows an overwhelming single-purchase rate, proving that top-of-funnel acquisition is functional but long-term retention is broken.
   * Re-allocating marketing budgets toward automated re-engagement during the critical 14 to 30-day window post-delivery captures customer intent before it drops off.
3. **Audit checkout UX for underused mid-range installment options before expanding credit policy.**
  * Consumer payment analysis indicates heavy reliance on credit cards, yet specific mid-range installment brackets remain underutilized due to interface friction.
  * Streamlining UI clarity and accessibility for these installment options fixes conversion barriers without risking broader credit policy changes.
4. **Recalibrate delivery-date estimation specifically for the five high-leak states, rather than treating lateness as purely a distance problem.**
  * The five pilot states (AL, MA, PI, CE, SE) experience severe SLA leakage that far exceeds standard distance-based logistics models.
  * Upgrading localized estimation algorithms closes the expectation-reality gap that destroys trust in these specific regions.
5. **Treat delivery reliability as a retention KPI, not just a logistics one.**
  * Supply chain execution directly dictates customer satisfaction, with late orders causing a steep review rating crash from 4.3 down to 2.6.
  * Elevating delivery reliability into a core retention metric aligns operational targets directly with lifetime value (LTV) protection.
